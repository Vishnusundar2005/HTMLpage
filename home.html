Vishnu

Vishnu is a principal deity in Hinduism, revered as the preserver and protector of the universe. He is one of the holy trinity, along with Brahma (the creator) and Shiva (the destroyer). Vishnu restores balance to the world by taking on avatars or incarnations, such as Rama and Krishna, to fight evil and maintain cosmic harmony, a role he will fulfill one last time to end the current age, according to belief.
